About MovieBook


MovieBook is a premium OTT discovery and streaming information platform designed to help users explore, discover, and stay updated with movies, web series, and exclusive content available across multiple streaming partners. Our mission is to bring entertainment lovers a unified experience where they can browse trending titles, explore detailed movie information, read community reviews, and find where to watch their favorite content without confusion.

We work closely with leading OTT platforms such as Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+ Hotstar, and JioCinema to showcase officially available content, trailers, and metadata. MovieBook does not host or stream copyrighted content directly. Instead, we act as an informational and discovery layer that redirects users to authorized streaming services.

MovieBook offers flexible subscription plans that unlock premium features such as personalized recommendations, watchlist syncing, early access to trending releases, and an ad-free browsing experience. Subscription plans may vary by region and are subject to change based on partner availability and service updates.

User privacy and data protection are extremely important to us. MovieBook follows strict privacy standards and does not sell or misuse personal data. Any information collected is used solely to improve user experience, enhance recommendations, and maintain platform security. By using MovieBook, you agree to our Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy.
